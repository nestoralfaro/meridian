## The short version

**Asset allocation** is how your money is divided between kinds of investments: mostly **stocks**, **bonds** and **cash**. Over long periods it explains most of the difference in how portfolios behave, more than which individual investments you pick.

## Why the mix matters

- **Stocks** have historically grown the most, with the biggest swings along the way.
- **Bonds** have usually grown less, but more steadily.
- **Cash** barely moves, which makes it useful for money you'll need soon.

A portfolio with more stocks will usually grow more over decades, and will also fall further in a bad year. Choosing a mix is choosing which of those you can live with.

## Where you see it in Meridian

Your dashboard's **allocation chart** groups everything you hold by asset class, with uninvested cash counted as cash. Its percentages always add up to exactly 100, and the table under the chart shows the same numbers.

## Rebalancing

Markets move your mix over time. If stocks have a great year, they become a bigger share than you planned. **Rebalancing** means moving money back to your intended mix, often once a year.
