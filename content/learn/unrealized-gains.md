## Cost basis

Your **cost basis** is what you paid for an investment, including any purchases you added later. It's the starting point for measuring how a holding has done.

## Unrealized gain or loss

An **unrealized gain** is the difference between what a holding is worth now and its cost basis, while you still own it:

> unrealized gain = market value − cost basis

It's "unrealized" because nothing has been sold. Prices keep moving, so the figure changes from day to day.

## Realized gains

When you sell, the gain or loss becomes **realized**. That's usually when it matters for taxes, depending on the account type. Gains inside retirement accounts such as IRAs are generally treated differently from gains in a regular brokerage account.

## Where you see it in Meridian

Open any account from your dashboard. The **Gain/loss** column shows each position's unrealized gain in dollars, and as a percentage of its cost basis.
